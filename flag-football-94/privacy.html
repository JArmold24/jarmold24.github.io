Privacy Policy

Flag Football '94 - effective November 6, 2026

Flag Football '94 is a football game you play on your own device, with optional head-to-head games against other players through Apple's Game Center. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no chat.

What stays on your device

Your settings, Superstar careers, Franchise leagues, custom plays and Hall of Fame are saved only on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game can:

  • post your scores to Game Center leaderboards (your retired Superstar's legacy score, your best Superstar season, the Skills drills, the Weekly Scenario and your head-to-head rating) and unlock Game Center achievements;
  • show those leaderboards and your achievements inside the game, with the Game Center nicknames and scores Apple returns for the top players and your friends;
  • send friend score Challenges through Game Center's own screens;
  • play a live head-to-head game against another player. During the game the two devices exchange only game data (the plays called, the controls pressed and the game state) through Game Center. Each player sees the other's Game Center nickname. There is no chat, voice or text.

Game Center is run by Apple; your Game Center nickname, friends, scores and match connections are handled under Apple's privacy policy. None of it is sent to us. You can play without Game Center, and you can turn it off at any time in the Settings app.

Age range

Where the law requires it (for example in Texas), the game asks Apple's Declared Age Range service, on your device, whether the player is an adult. If the player is under 18 and a parent's consent is required, the online features (head to head and friend Challenges) stay locked until a parent approves them through a request Apple sends to the parent; every offline mode works without approval. The game only checks whether the player is an adult, while it runs. It never stores or sends anyone's age or birthday, and Apple's age range service is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Purchases

The app is free to download and offers one in-app purchase, the Full Game. Purchases are made and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The game only checks with Apple, on your device, whether the Full Game has been bought, so it can unlock it.

Children

The game collects no information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents can turn off Game Center multiplayer and friend features for a child in Screen Time (Content & Privacy Restrictions > Game Center).

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
